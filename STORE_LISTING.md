## Long description 詳述
EN:
Qwen for Excel and Word brings a Copilot-style task pane to Excel and Word. Chat answers from your selection or active sheet and can review the entire workbook read-only (sheet inventory, batched profiles, per-voucher debit/credit balance, export-vs-ledger cross-checks, formula-error scan). Plan proposes changes; Agent executes approved steps with independently computed finance checks; destructive edits always wait for a click and Undo reverses applied changes. A Copilot-style sidebar holds Guide, workbook context, cloud sources, saved skills, history and memory on the local edition; the hosted edition ships core + Guide only. Bring your own QwenCloud / Alibaba Cloud Model Studio key: it stays on your PC (local relay) or in your browser (hosted, sent per request, never stored server-side). You pay your provider directly; the add-in is free.

繁中:
Qwen for Excel and Word 為 Excel／Word 帶來 Copilot 式工作窗格。Chat 依選取範圍或作用中工作表回答，并可整本唯讀覆核（工作表目錄、分批摘要、每批次借貸平衡、匯出對總帳交叉核對、公式錯誤掃描）。Plan 提出修改；Agent 執行已批准步驟並附獨立計算之財務檢查；破壞性修改必待點擊，Undo 可復原。Copilot 式側欄於本機版容纳 Guide、活頁簿上下文、雲端來源、已存技能、歷史與記憶；託管版僅核心＋Guide。自備 QwenCloud／阿里雲百煉金鑰：本機存於您的 PC（loopback relay），託管存於瀏覽器、每請求傳送、伺服器絕不儲存。費用直接付予供應商；增益集免費。
