Privacy Policy · 私隱政策

Last updated 更新: 2026-11-24 · Applies to the "Qwen for Excel and Word" Office add-in (local relay and hosted pane). 適用於本增益集（本機 relay 與託管 pane）。

1. What is sent, and when · 傳送內容與時機

  • Nothing is sent until you press Send. The Share toggle is a consent gate: off = no workbook/document data leaves the pane. 未按 Send 不傳送；Share 關閉＝不傳送活頁簿資料。
  • With Share on: your question, the selected range or active-sheet used range (Chat), or the sheet inventory plus ranges the Agent requests (Agent/Plan), and any attachment text you added. Share 開啟時：問題、選取範圍／作用中工作表（Chat）、或 Agent 要求的工作表目錄與範圍、以及您加入的附件文字。
  • Local-only features (OneDrive search, Saved skills, History, Memory, PDF/XLSX/image extraction) run on your PC via the local relay; their stored data never leaves it except as attachment/skill text you send. 本機功能之儲存資料除您傳送者外不離機。
  • Scanned PDFs/images: up to 12 rendered pages are sent to QwenCloud for transcription using your own API key, labelled in the attachment. 掃描檔最多 12 頁以您自己的 key 送 QwenCloud 轉錄並標示。

2. Your API key (BYO) · 您的金鑰

  • You supply your own Qwen (Alibaba Cloud Model Studio / QwenCloud) key. Local mode: it is held by the loopback relay on this PC (127.0.0.1) and optionally in this browser's local storage. Hosted mode: it stays in this browser and is sent per request; the Worker forwards it to QwenCloud and never stores or logs it. 自備金鑰；本機存於 loopback relay；託管每請求傳送、Worker 絕不儲存或記錄。
  • Keys are never written to the repository, the audit card, or logs. 金鑰不入 repo、audit 卡或記錄。

3. Where data goes · 資料去向

  • Pane → your local relay (local mode) or the Cloudflare Worker (hosted mode) → QwenCloud chat-completions endpoint you configure. Model responses return the same path. 路徑：pane→relay/Worker→QwenCloud。
  • OneDrive search (local mode, optional) sends your query to Microsoft Graph under your own Microsoft sign-in; previews stay in the pane. OneDrive 搜尋以您自己的 Microsoft 登入送查詢；預覽留 pane。
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking. 無分析、無廣告、無第三方追蹤。

4. Retention · 保留

  • Conversation history, memory notes and saved sessions live in per-user local app data on your PC (newest 100 sessions); delete them from the History card or by removing the folder. 對話／記憶／session 存本機每用戶目錄（最新 100）；可於 History 刪除。
  • The hosted Worker keeps no conversation or key state between requests. 託管 Worker 不保留請求間狀態。
  • Provider-side retention follows your QwenCloud account terms. 供應商端保留依您 QwenCloud 帳戶條款。
